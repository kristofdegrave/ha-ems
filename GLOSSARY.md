# Glossary

The shared language of ha-ems. One entry per term; edit via the `domain-modeling` skill.

- **Solar surplus**: solar production minus house consumption; the power available for charging without drawing from the grid.
- **Tariff**: the price of grid energy at a given time, import or export.
- **Home battery**: the stationary battery that stores and releases energy for the house.
- **Charge session**: one EV charging run, from plug-in to unplug.
